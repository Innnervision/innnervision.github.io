Badminton Smash Pro Tips: Timing Jump Smashes & Net Drops

  1. Force your opponent to the back baseline with a high clear lob before executing a deceptive net drop shot.
  2. Trigger your jump smash at the apex of the shuttlecock arc for maximum downward velocity.
