How to Master Cue Spin & Bank Shots in 8 Ball Pool Legends

  1. Use top-spin (follow) when you need the cue ball to roll forward after impact to set up your next pocket.
  2. Apply back-spin (draw) on straight shots to pull the cue ball backward toward the center of the baize.
  3. Aim through cushion diamonds for precision bank shots when an opponent ball blocks the direct pocket lane.
